The Application can both read from and write to connected QuickBooks companies. Write
operations create real accounting records — including customers, invoices, bills, items,
chart-of-accounts entries, and bulk transaction imports — and some operations transmit
email to recipients recorded in QuickBooks.

**You are solely responsible for reviewing records created through the Application and for
any communication it sends on your behalf.** The Provider recommends testing all write
operations against an Intuit sandbox company before connecting production books.
