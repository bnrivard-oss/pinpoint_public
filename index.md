# Claude Pnpt MCP

A private QuickBooks Online integration operated by **Pinpoint Family Office Ltd.**

This application connects QuickBooks Online to internal accounting workflows through the
Model Context Protocol. It is not a public product: it is used solely by authorised
personnel of Pinpoint Family Office Ltd., is not distributed, and is not offered for sale.

## What it does

The application reads financial data from authorised QuickBooks Online companies —
profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, aged receivables and payables, invoices, and
ad-hoc queries — and can create accounting records including customers, invoices, bills,
items, and chart-of-accounts entries.

It runs locally on the operator's own machine. There is no hosted service, no server-side
database, and no stored copy of any accounting data.
